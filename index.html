Act 3 · Offensive

Offensive security is authorised assurance: each test answers a different question

Vulnerability scan

Finds possible weaknesses across many assets, quickly and repeatedly.

Click to flip

Cannot tell you

Whether the weakness is exploitable in your environment, or what an attacker could reach with it.

Penetration test

Validates exploitability within an agreed scope and time window.

Click to flip

Cannot tell you

Whether your SOC would notice, or what happens outside the scope and the test dates.

Red team

Tests whether an adversary can achieve an objective, and whether you detect and respond.

Click to flip

Cannot tell you

Every weakness that exists. It follows one realistic path, not full coverage.

AI red team

Tests prompts, retrieval, tools, agents and guardrails for injection and excessive agency.

Click to flip

Cannot tell you

That the model is safe next month. Models, data and prompts change; testing must repeat.

Kabir · red team lead
No signed scope, no test. My report is worth nothing if it breaks production or touches real customer data.
Rules before any test: written authorisation · scope · rules of engagement · safe stop conditions · evidence handling · remediation-focused reporting
